End-to-End Walkthrough Steps

1 Check Connection Status
The status pill above confirms local loopback binding. Shows whether Gemini AI reasoning is online or running in offline mock mode.
2 Send a Text Command
Type or click a quick chip below (e.g. "What is the system status?" or "Show my demo notes"). Jarvis executes safe registered tools.
3 Open Chrome Demo Page & Extension
Open /demo/ in Google Chrome, click the Jarvis toolbar extension icon, and select "Read this page".
4 Inspect Extracted Text & Disclosure
The extension displays character count and clarifies that text is sent to Gemini for grounded summarization or practice quiz study assistance.
5 Push-to-Talk Voice Input
Tap the microphone button, speak your request, inspect and edit the transcript in the input box, then send.
6 Active Stop / Pause Control
During any in-flight agent processing, click the red Stop button to immediately abort via AbortController.
7 One-Step Cleanup
Tap "Clear Demo Data" above at any time to purge mock demo notes and reminders without affecting real user data.
