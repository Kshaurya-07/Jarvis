01 · Companion

One companion.
Every device.

Jarvis is a personal AI companion that helps across your paired devices — not just on one laptop. Give a command on your phone, your laptop, or your tablet, and choose where it should happen.

Jarvis understands the request, picks the permitted device, explains what it plans to do, does only what is allowed, and reports the result back to you.

02 · Devices

Say it here. It happens there.

The long-term vision is simple: the device you speak to does not have to be the device that does the work. Today, the phone-to-laptop direction works when your phone is paired with your laptop. The other directions are on the roadmap, and they are labelled honestly below.

Phone → Laptop

“Laptop par yeh file kholo aur summarize karo.”

  1. Phone You speak or type the command in the companion app.
  2. Jarvis Reads the file — only inside your approved workspace — and explains the plan before acting.
  3. Laptop The summary is prepared on your laptop and reported back to your phone.

Available Works today with a paired phone and laptop.

Laptop → Phone

“Mere phone par reminder set karo.”

  1. Laptop You ask Jarvis on your laptop to set a reminder on your phone.
  2. Jarvis Would need a companion running on the phone that can receive actions — not just send them.
  3. Phone The reminder would appear on your phone, with the same confirmation rules as on the laptop.

Planned Reminders today are stored on the laptop only. Phone-side actions are not built yet.

Every handoff depends on the target device being paired, online, and permitted to do that action. If any of those is missing, Jarvis says so instead of pretending it worked.

03 · How it works

Four steps. Nothing hidden.

01

Understand the request

Jarvis reads what you asked — typed or spoken — and works out what you want and which device it belongs to. If the request is unclear, it asks instead of guessing.

02

Choose a permitted device

It checks which of your devices is paired, online, and allowed to do that action. A device that is offline or not permitted is never used silently.

03

Perform supported actions

Only approved actions run, and anything that changes something asks for your confirmation first. You can stop a running task at any time.

04

Show the result

The result comes back to the device you asked from, with a short record of what was done in the activity history.

04 · Capabilities

What Jarvis can do — and what it can’t yet.

Every capability below is labelled with its real status in the project today. “Available” means the code for it exists and is tested; anything else is marked as it is.

Chat & voice

Gemini chat and natural question-answering, with push-to-talk voice commands, an editable transcript, and optional spoken replies. Voice recognition runs in your browser; raw audio is never sent to the server.

Available

Paired devices

Pair your phone with your laptop using a one-time 6-digit code. See connection status, task progress, and activity history, and stop a running task. Today one laptop can be paired at a time; choosing between several devices is next.

Available Multi-device picker — In development

Device help

Launch apps from an approved allowlist and check laptop battery, storage, and memory. Windows-only tools report “unsupported” on macOS and Linux instead of failing silently.

Available

Screen & browser help

On your request, Jarvis can read the page you are on, summarise it, take a screenshot, and help with practice questions for study. It never submits forms, quizzes, exams, or purchases.

Available

Files, notes & memory

Files are restricted to your approved workspace and notes folders. You control what Jarvis remembers — memories are saved only when you approve them, and you can delete any of them.

Available

Reminders & briefings

Local reminders stored on your laptop, and an on-demand daily briefing that summarises reminders, notes, and a web overview. Reminders on your phone are planned, not available yet.

Available — laptop Planned — phone

Web search

Search the web through official search APIs, with source links in the results. Jarvis does not scrape websites, and it never sends credentials to a search provider.

Available

Google, read-only

Optional Gmail and Calendar summaries after you sign in with Google’s official OAuth flow. Read-only: Jarvis cannot send mail, delete messages, or create or edit events.

Requires permission

Your data, your control

Export your data, delete it, or take a checksum-verified local backup — all from your laptop, with a confirmation step before any deletion.

Available

06 · Privacy & control

Your laptop is the boundary.

Local by design

The Jarvis backend runs on your own laptop and binds to localhost by default. There is no cloud relay, no telemetry, and no background sync. Your phone reaches it only over your private Tailscale network, after pairing.

Permissions first

Apps can only be launched from an allowlist, files only inside approved folders, and browser actions only on domains you approve. Sensitive fields — passwords, cards, tokens — are never read or filled.

Confirmations

Anything that changes something — opening an app, deleting a reminder, clearing data — creates a one-time confirmation you approve or deny. You can stop a running task at any time.

What leaves your laptop

Chat text and the content you ask to summarise are sent to Gemini; search queries go to your chosen search provider; Gmail and Calendar data goes to Gemini only when you ask for a summary, and only after you sign in with Google. Passwords and API keys are never stored in memory or logs — and you should never share them in chat.

Logs you can read

Every action Jarvis takes is written to an audit log on your laptop, with secrets and file contents redacted. You can open it from the companion app and export or delete your data at any time.
